# Add-ons and other Perks <!-- markdownlint-disable-line -->

### Become an Afterparty Sponsor <!-- markdownlint-disable-line -->

You can bring a rollup to the afterparty and provide other swag placed on the tables for the afterparty.\
Your logo and message on rotation on a screen at the venue
Create and name your own cocktail for the afterparty.\
You choose the music for the afterparty!\
Guaranteed Tickets: 2\
Logo on the website\
Cost: 750\* Euro (Add-on) or 1500 Euro (afterparty only)\
Slots: 2\
*not available for community sponsor level

### Become a Swag Bag Sponsor <!-- markdownlint-disable-line -->

Provide swag bags for the event with a logo of your choice on one side and the BSidesVienna logo on the other side.\
You can also give us a logo for one side and provide the funding, we do the rest.\
Guaranteed Tickets: 5\
Logo on the website\
Cost: 450 swag bags or TBD\
Slots: 1

### Become a Lanyard Sponsor <!-- markdownlint-disable-line -->

Provide lanyards for the event with a logo of your choice or work with us to create a lanyard design and provide the funding.\
Guaranteed Tickets: 5\
Logo on the website\
Cost: 450 lanyards or around EUR 1500 - 1900\
Slots: 1
